LeagueSwitcher

Five directions built from things you actually see in the game — the Rift, a Nexus, a poro, a Hextech gem, a ward — each carrying the idea of exchange. Shown large, then at 48 / 32 / 16 px and on a light tile, because Start-menu search renders small. Tell me a number and I'll build it.

Riot's own logo and rank crests are deliberately not used — they're trademarked art, and this is going in a public repo.

01Summoner's Rift

The minimap: the square, the river running one diagonal, and mid lane running the other — from the blue base to the red one. That lane already connects two opposite sides, so it becomes the swap axis. The most League-specific shape there is that isn't Riot's own art.

02Nexus Pair

Two Nexus crystals, blue and gold, one raised over the other. You destroy one to win and defend the other; here they're the two accounts, and the arrows move between them.

03Poro Swap

A poro, split down the middle into two colours. No non-player will read it as League, but every player will, instantly — and it's the only option here with any warmth to it.

04Hextech Gem

The faceted gem off a Hextech chest, cut into a blue half and a gold half with the swap through the middle. Angular enough to stay sharp when it shrinks.

05Ward Totem

A stealth ward — the eye on a stake you drop in a bush. It's the item you place to see somewhere you aren't, which is close enough to “look at another account” to be a joke worth making.

Pick a number — or mix, e.g. “04 but in the blue of 01”. I'll render it to build/icon.png at 512 px, rebuild the portable exe with it, and it becomes the taskbar and Start-menu icon.

Drawn as flat vectors on purpose: Windows renders this at 16 px in the taskbar and in Start search results, where gradients and fine detail turn to mush. Each one is legible as a silhouette first.
